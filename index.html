How a book moves through the studio

Draft in. Book out.

  1. Step 1

    Send the manuscript

    Share your draft as it is, along with where you want the book to go. We read it and agree a plan and timeline with you.

  2. Step 2

    Edit

    Your editor works through the text with tracked changes and notes. You stay in charge of every decision.

  3. Step 3

    Format

    The clean text is set into a print interior and a reflowable ebook, with consistent styles from title page to index.

  4. Step 4

    Design the cover

    Cover concepts, then refinements, then a full wrap sized to your final page count so the spine fits.

  5. Step 5

    Publish and launch

    Your print and ebook editions go live, and your author website and blog are ready to send readers to them.
