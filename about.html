Our vision

No good book should stay hidden in a drafts folder.

Kodsinc Studio exists so that a story written at a kitchen table can stand proudly beside anything from a big publishing house. We want every author, whatever their experience or budget, to hold a book they love, and to have a home online where readers can find them and the person behind the stories.

  • Publishing that feels personalOne team from first draft to launch day, who knows your book almost as well as you do.
  • Indie books that stand tallCovers, interiors and files made to professional standards, so readers judge your book by its story.
  • Authors who keep growingWebsites and blogs that keep readers finding you long after launch week.

Working together

Your story, your voice, your call.

I know how much heart, creativity, late nights and probably far too much coffee go into writing a book. You shouldn't have to figure out every technical detail on your own, and you should never lose your voice along the way.

  • You stay in chargeEvery edit is a suggestion. The final word on your story is always yours.
  • Plain answers, no jargonISBNs, trim sizes, bleed and KDP categories, explained simply, so you always know what's happening and why.
  • Someone who knows your bookYou work with people who have actually read your manuscript, not a ticket queue.
  • Honest from day oneClear feedback, clear timelines and no surprises about what's included.
